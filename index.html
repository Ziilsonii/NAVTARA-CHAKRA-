Moon position is computed with a compact astronomical approximation (accurate to roughly ±1°, i.e. within a couple of hours of the true nakshatra change). For a Tara falling within a few hours of a boundary, cross-check against a Panchang before acting on it.

Janma nakshatra and Tara groupings follow standard Parashari Navtara counting. Deity, animal, tree and direction are standard nakshatra attributes; direction is derived from each nakshatra's planetary lord. Predictions and remedies are traditional generalisations, not a substitute for a full chart reading.
